Audio gesture patterns are authored animation parameters, not learned psychological measurements. Package wheels and model weights keep their own upstream licenses.

### Medium speech reference and synchronized motion

- LAION Humaneness Ears **Medium**, same pinned model revision above, safetensors SHA-256 `00f30de0f7d656d2a1e86bdc629673ff8b576532e1263b02bcebdfc2763e54f1`, 388,447,068 bytes. Its score-only TorchScript export is parity-checked separately from Base.
- Attribution: **LAION — Procedural Voice Captions / Humaneness Ears Medium integration**, [repository](https://github.com/LAION-AI/procedural-voice-captions), revision `7ebd4a9b7da96df989af8fc6ad0ce34f4e8aa3ed`. `baseline_stats_humaneness_medium_speech.json` is retained unmodified as `models/ears/medium/speech_reference.json`; CC BY 4.0, with the upstream notice in `REFERENCE-LICENSE.md`. It contains equal-per-clip arithmetic means and population standard deviations from 80,265 S10/Emilia clips. The app adds its own z-based animation selection, style modulation and browser motion; these are not upstream caption rules or validated psychological measurements. Medium statistics are never silently applied to Base predictions.
- Webcam eye-position tracking during playback reuses the existing local MediaPipe model. No camera frames, face embeddings or viewer identity are retained.

## Expression models added to the Windows application

- [Empathic Insight Face Small](https://huggingface.co/laion/Empathic-Insight-Face-Small), LAION, CC-BY-4.0, revision `11563ec072a72fddf1be992b9abc0a1c675cf75a`.
- [Empathic Insight Face Large](https://huggingface.co/laion/Empathic-Insight-Face-Large), LAION, CC-BY-4.0, revision `1b2e950bbc35f11407de7e92bd5d70d6fd5067ee`.
- Attribution: Christoph Schuhmann and coauthors, *EmoNet-Face: An Expert-Annotated Benchmark for Synthetic Emotion Recognition* (2025), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20033), [official repository](https://github.com/laion-ai/emonet-face).
- Each downloaded head has a recorded SHA-256 in `models/empathic/source.json` or `models/empathic/small/source.json`. Conversion to NumPy preserves the weights and is checked against PyTorch with a normalized test embedding. Small's four additional scalar dimensions are excluded from the 40-category rig taxonomy.
- [SigLIP2 SO400M patch16 384](https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-so400m-patch16-384), Google, Apache-2.0. The vision-only [ONNX Community export](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/siglip2-so400m-patch16-384-ONNX) is pinned to revision `29f74e673c298fd07482d8e20138179282001eb7`. CPU INT8 convolution weights and their zero point are shifted together by 128 and stored unsigned; centered values are unchanged. The derived checksum and transformation are recorded in `cpu_encoder.source.json`. Q4 was also benchmarked locally. Neither quantized export has been validated here for equivalent human-emotion accuracy to full precision.
- [OpenCV Zoo facial-expression recognition](https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/facial_expression_recognition), MobileFaceNet / Progressive Teacher, Apache-2.0. The local model's provenance and checksum are recorded in `models/expression_mobilefacenet.source.json`; its license is `models/expression_LICENSE`.
- The 40 original emotion recipes and 60 separately labeled social cartoon recipes are from [christophschuhmann/cartoon-face-generator](https://github.com/christophschuhmann/cartoon-face-generator), MIT, and share its 101-point topology and Python renderer.

The seven embedded example images and rounded prediction tables in LAION's published `EmoNet-Face-Small.html` are used only for local model-output reproduction checks. That check is not an independent human-emotion validation and does not establish real-webcam accuracy.

The application and bundled `cartoon_face` renderer source use the repository's MIT license, included at the bundle root as `LICENSE`.

MediaPipe is installed from PyPI as `mediapipe==0.10.35`. Its code is distributed under Apache 2.0. The task model is the pinned version-1 official Google Face Landmarker bundle, not a model trained by this project. The download script checks its SHA-256 and writes a provenance sidecar. Model cards and usage information are linked from the official [Face Landmarker overview](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker). The model card terms govern the model; the repository's MIT license does not relicense it.

- Model URL: `https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task`
- SHA-256: `64184e229b263107bc2b804c6625db1341ff2bb731874b0bcc2fe6544e0bc9ff`
- Size: 3,758,596 bytes.
- [MediaPipe Python task guide](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/python) documents the Tasks API, CPU delegate, timestamps, face mesh and subject-side blendshape names.
- [MediaPipe code license](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/LICENSE).

The verification photograph is NASA's astronaut Eileen Collins image, obtained through `skimage.data.astronaut()`. The [scikit-image documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut) identifies it as public domain with no known copyright restrictions. The photograph is used only for reproducible local verification. One photograph and its crops/repeated frames are one human subject. The laptop source ZIP does not include this photograph or recorded camera input.

Other runtime dependencies retain their upstream licenses: NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, CairoSVG, Flask, and MediaPipe's transitive dependencies including OpenCV. They are installed by pip; their third-party source is not vendored into the ZIP. The native Cairo runtime is required by the existing renderer. See [CairoSVG installation documentation](https://cairosvg.org/documentation/).
