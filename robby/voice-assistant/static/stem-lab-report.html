Robby: source separation pilot

GPU update: TUSS now defaults to CUDA on GPU 0. A concurrent full-lab pilot measured 916 ms for its first 3-second separation and 120 ms for the optional second pass, with 8.2 s total wall time. An isolated warmed model measured about 55 ms per 3-second window; loading and competing GPU work are excluded. GPU memory is shared and tightly constrained.

The lab adds optional source separation alongside MOSS Audio 4B and LFM3 video. TUSS or Bandit produces estimated speech, music and effects; each can be played. A shared sound captioner describes the stems; the already-active Parakeet and Humaneness Ears analyze speech. They may be busy and may return incorrect results.

In a single generated 3-s mixture with two synthetic voices, music and impulses, TUSS improved a basic same-signal SDR from 1.77 to 6.15 dB for speech, -5.03 to 12.21 dB for music and -7.15 to 24.63 dB for effects. This illustrative test is not a human listening study or a general quality claim.

Windows are processed independently. A 1-second stride causes repeated work: at the measured CPU timing, the separator alone needs about four seconds of compute for each second of new audio. Word timestamps, stable speaker identity and speaker embeddings are not implemented in this branch. Weak or silent stems can contain artifacts and may trigger false captions.
